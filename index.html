How to Use PlastiScan Pro

Plastic Identification & Pollution Scanning Guide

1. Optical Camera Scanner Mode

Click Launch Camera Optical Scan. Point your device camera at a plastic item inside the square reticle field. The system analyzes visual refractive characteristics and optical opacity to classify plastics by Society of the Plastics Industry (SPI) Resin Identification Codes (#1 through #7).

2. Resin Codes & Identification

Plastics are categorized into 7 standardized codes (PETE, HDPE, PVC, LDPE, PP, PS, OTHER). The app estimates material density ($g/cm^3$), eco-decomposition timeframe, flammability, and recycling viability.

3. Polymer Synth Audio Feedback

Enable sound feedback to hear acoustic pitch cues. High density materials (like PVC or PET) generate distinct higher-frequency synthetic tones, while organics remain silent.

4. Interactive Beach Cleanup Simulator

Switch to Beach Hunt Simulation mode or scroll to the field grid. Drag the detector probe across the soil or sand to locate microplastics and ocean trash buried beneath organic shells and driftwood.
